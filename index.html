      What is a spin-the-wheel picker?

      A spin-the-wheel picker is an online random selector: each entry you add becomes a slice of a wheel, and when you spin, the slice that stops under the pointer is the result. Because everyone can watch the wheel turn, the choice feels open and fair in a way a hidden random number never does.

      Questions

      Is the wheel truly random?

      Yes. Every spin uses your browser's cryptographic random number generator (crypto.getRandomValues), and each spin is independent of the last. Getting the same result twice is normal, just like flipping heads twice.

      Do I need an account?

      No. Your entries are saved automatically in your browser.

      Can I remove a winner after the spin?

      Yes. Turn on "Remove winner after each spin" and the picked entry leaves the wheel automatically.

      How many entries can I add?

      As many as you like. Labels get smaller as you add more; for readability most people use up to 50.

      Can I use Wheelova for giveaways?

      Yes. Spin live in front of your audience. Giveaway rules differ by country, so check what applies to you.
